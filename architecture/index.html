The six stages

Every forecast-driving value follows one path

1

Profile

Cover business, products, segments, calendar, drivers, accounting, guidance, seasonality and exposures.

2

Map

Declare material signals, evidence, direction, period, formula and correlation group.

3

Resolve

Search only approved signals and propose exact-quote typed observations.

4

Validate

Check hash, cutoff, quote, unit, period, basis, decimals and duplication.

5

Compile

Calculate the base forecast and conditional scenarios with Decimal.

6

Challenge

Reject leakage, stale evidence, false precision and unreconciled values.

Validation contract

Confidence never promotes a fact

Deterministic acceptance

  • Known, pre-cutoff publication time
  • Frozen bytes match SHA-256
  • Exact quote exists in those bytes
  • Signal is approved for the metric
  • Period, units and basis match
  • Numbers remain decimal strings

Automatic rejection

  • URL or consensus without frozen proof
  • Missing or post-cutoff source time
  • Actual presented as a forecast
  • Arbitrary qualitative weight
  • Correlated driver double-count
  • Incomplete/error look-ahead review

Quality control

The handoff is a gate, not a copy step

  • Every profile section has source-backed claims
  • Every metric has 3–7 signals and one anchor
  • Role, resolver and formula combinations are supported
  • Accepted observations reconstruct from frozen evidence
  • Research audit matches the canonical source manifest
  • Independent no-web review has passed
  • Modifiers stay qualitative and scenarios conditional
  • Rejected signals preserve the anchor or baseline

Fail closed: missing or invalid evidence cannot move a forecast.

Reproducibility

Two bounded research commands

Profile research

npm run research:profiles

Builds nine-section source candidate bundles for all four companies.

Approved-signal research

npm run research:signals

Reads validated maps from signal_maps/ and searches only declared evidence requirements.

Known limitations

What the system does not claim

Discovery is fallible

Tavily or a model may miss material evidence. Missing sections and unresolved URLs remain explicit.

Review is bounded

The reviewer finds unsupported output; it cannot prove whether knowledge came from model training.

Accuracy is separate

Replayability and provenance do not guarantee forecast accuracy. Company-specific judgement still matters.
